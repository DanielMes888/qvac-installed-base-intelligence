# Product grilling record

Status: Grilling complete; ready for `/to-spec`. This is a decision and validation record, not a technical specification. No implementation is authorized by this record. Read alongside OFFICIAL_PROJECT_PLAN.md; assigned experiments are gates rather than achieved results.

## Settled decisions — round 1

- D1 (Q1): Primary MVP persona is a hypothetical field Account Manager / Sales Representative. The scenario is a short natural-language note immediately after a hospital visit, after selecting the hospital. Information may come from conversations or a walkthrough; identifiers, complete inventory, and installation dates may be unavailable.
- D2 (Q2): The reviewed observation serves as a useful in-app visit note, accompanied by a structured customer snapshot, verification list, structured export, and previous observations for future visits. Replacing a required Philips report or integrating with corporate systems is not claimed. Structured export is explicitly included in the MVP.
- D3 (Q3): Intended responsibility for unresolved information lies with the account owner or next visitor. No dedicated data-steward role is introduced. Complex conflicts may remain unresolved; retained evidence and visible uncertainty still provide value. How another employee accesses local data remains open.
- D4 (Q4): The working view is not Philips' official system of record. Identified asset counts and reported totals remain separate; the dashboard defaults to identified assets and never adds reported totals to them. User example: three identified records, reported total four, unresolved difference one. Eligibility for the identified count, comparability of scope/dates, and whether the difference implies a missing asset remain open. The user proposed displaying the latest reported total; handling competing reports requires refinement.
- D5 (Q5): An observation of three DemoScan Technologies MRI scanners may include age and location claims about one unidentified member. Preserve that member-level scope; do not copy its fields to all three or choose an existing asset arbitrarily. A later link requires sufficient evidence, whose threshold is not yet defined. The fictional manufacturer name reflects D27's later synthetic-universe rule.

## Settled decisions — round 2

These refine D1-D4 where the initial wording was broader. The official plan now incorporates settled decisions through round 4; entries below preserve the discussion history and are superseded where later decisions explicitly say so. Any subsequent changes must also be reconciled before /to-spec.

- D6 (Q6): Single-user, single-device MVP. The same account manager captures notes, reads local history and verification items, and exports structured records. Fictional employee histories demonstrate prior evidence, not cross-device collaboration. Synchronization, shared accounts, backend storage, and CRM ingestion are outside scope. Replace immediate corporate-visibility claims with: "The prototype demonstrates how locally captured evidence can produce a structured installed-base view and an exportable record suitable for future corporate integration." Export timing/transfer remains to be clarified because local file creation need not require connectivity.
- D7 (Q7): Replace "identified asset" with verified individual asset (durable identifier/reference), provisional individual asset (contextually distinguishable unit), and unlinked equipment claim (distinct unit not established). Show verified assets, provisional assets, and reported totals separately; never present their sum as authoritative inventory. Serial/manufacturer qualifies for the verified category only after validation without conflict; room/model evidence is provisional; vague new-equipment reports stay unlinked. The required identifier validation is unresolved, especially against D9's rule for manually entered serials. Approval of an interpretation is not identity evidence or confirmation.
- D8 (Q8): Subtract counts only when customer, site/campus, applicable department/organization scope, modality, relevant time basis, and count meaning are comparable. Preserve value, type, location scope, as-of/observation date, source, and certainty. Use "Count discrepancy: 1" rather than implying one missing asset. For incompatible scopes, show both values and "Scope unresolved" without subtraction. Competing totals remain visible; recency does not settle a contradiction or replace evidence history. Unknown quantity scope and rules for legitimate changes over time still require explicit treatment.
- D9 (Q9): Source type and certainty are separate. Sources include direct observation, attributed staff statement, unattributed statement, existing authorized record, and supporting document/equipment label. Certainty remains Reported, Estimated, Confirmed, or Unknown. Preserve provided attribution. New text capture normally does not confer Confirmed; it requires inspectable evidence under explicit rules. A typed serial alone is reported and establishes a proposed identity; seeded records may already be confirmed. Repeated agreement does not promote certainty. "Another explicitly defined verification mechanism" is not yet a defined rule, and support for new documents/labels has not been approved. The example classifying "there are three" as a reported total still needs a completeness/scope rule; staff attribution alone does not establish either.
- D10 (Q10): Capture an equipment observation note, not a complete sales or service visit note. Input wording: "Describe the medical equipment you observed or were told about." Preserve the equipment narrative and supporting excerpts; do not structure commercial commitments, meeting summaries, service actions, patient information, or general customer discussions. Claim reduced effort to turn an equipment observation into structured installed-base evidence, not replacement of a complete note/CRM activity/service report. Behavior when unrelated or sensitive text is nevertheless entered remains open.

## Settled decisions — round 3 and platform clarification

- D11 (Q11; corrects D7): Identifier validation checks syntax, and uniqueness checks local collisions. Neither verifies a real physical unit. This synthetic MVP verifies identity only by matching an explicitly trusted fictional seeded reference. New serials remain reported; identity verification does not confirm age, location, condition, or current installation. The demonstration is synthetic, not a real-world verification service.
- D12 (Q12): Repeated contextual descriptions suggest existing provisional records; observations remain separate until an explicit reconciliation decision. The user may link provisionally, create a separate provisional record, or leave unresolved. Default to unresolved when evidence is insufficient. Record decision, actor, date, and reason via a short selector and optional text. Accepting extraction and accepting a provisional match are distinct; neither verifies identity. Unresolved observations do not grow provisional-record counts, whose labels must not claim distinct equipment. Reconciliation can be deferred.
- D13 (Q13; corrects D9's example): Quantity scope is observed, reported total, or unknown. A total requires explicit completeness language or a clarifying answer; location scope is independently established before assigning it to a hospital/campus/department. Hospital selection establishes customer context only. "The manager said there are three" retains value three with quantity scope unknown. Ask about it only if it is the highest-priority clarification.
- D14 (Q14): Preserve recorded time and explicitly supplied effective time without inventing missing dates. Use potential conflict for incompatible comparable scope/period claims, possible change for unexplained differences over time, and reported change/correction for an explicit transition/revision. An explicit correction may supersede a prior claim; an explicit change may establish a newer reported state. Retain both history and change evidence. Recency alone does not resolve disagreement or retire assets. Display approximate ages with evidence dates and do not flag normal aging solely from differing age numbers. More complex temporal reconciliation is manual; automatic asset lifecycle management is outside MVP. Record counts are not an audited current inventory.
- D15 (Q15): Preserve original notes unchanged. Date and attribute each correction/follow-up and relate it to the original observation. Extraction corrections cite the original excerpt; new/revised assertions cite the new evidence entry. Retain history and appropriate explicit supersession. Changing an estimated age from eight to five does not make it exact or Confirmed. Use a lightweight evidence-entry model, claim references, and explicit reconciliation decisions; exclude a general-purpose knowledge graph and full event-sourcing framework.
- D16 (platform clarification): Platform is undecided pending technical validation. Mobile is an initial preference, not a challenge requirement or settled product decision; desktop and web are valid alternatives from the outset. Choose using QVAC feasibility, available hardware, development effort, offline usability, and reliable end-to-end demonstration. The first spike validates QVAC on the selected execution hardware, which need not be a phone. All evaluated inference uses @qvac/sdk on-device or via permitted peer-to-peer delegation, without cloud inference. A browser UI must name its QVAC execution host and must not be presented as proof of browser-local inference. Permitted delegation and its network/privacy behavior require validation before selection; inference delegation does not imply workspace synchronization.

Resolve the documented Node requirement and verify runtime/backend compatibility before assuming acceleration. Record available memory and actual backend during execution. - D18 (Q17; supersedes D16's peer option): Exclude delegated inference from the MVP. QVAC, the workspace, and any browser UI must run on the same physical computer. Do not downgrade the SDK to recover delegation; pin the tested version and cite the official release notes removing it. Peer model distribution is not peer inference. See ADR 0003.
- D19 (Q18): Assign the first spike to Codex for implementation/results, with the project owner handling local device interaction and reviewing findings. Execute only through the later approved spike ticket. Timebox active implementation to three hours, track dependency/model downloads separately, and report total elapsed time. Use twenty predefined representative notes without example-specific prompt tuning, run offline after setup, and apply the E4 gates below. A failed gate triggers a stop, concrete blocker report, and smallest proposed model/runtime/platform change; no silent threshold relaxation or indefinite tuning.
- D20 (Q19): Persist the original note before inference. Track extraction independently as Not started, Processing, Succeeded, or Failed. Model-load failure, timeout, or unusable output retains the note, explains saved-note/extraction-failure status, and offers retry or manual structured entry. Invalid machine output never enters customer views/aggregates. Manual claims have human provenance and appropriate certainty, and do not count as successful AI extraction. Separately report first-attempt success, post-retry success, manual recovery, and unresolved failures. A demo failure must be shown honestly; manual recovery demonstrates resilience but does not satisfy QVAC extraction demonstration.
- D21 (Q20): Application rules select the clarification target; QVAC identifies ambiguities and phrases a question about that target. Priority is material quantity completeness/location scope/claim attachment, then identity details that distinguish actual candidates, then optional model/manufacturer/age information. Ask only when materially useful, at most once per capture; answer, I don't know, or skip/save must all be possible. Preserve an answer as additional evidence and do not ask again after processing it. If generation fails, use a predefined question for the selected target or finish without one. Full inference-call/timing budgets beyond the spike remain to be settled.

## Settled decisions — round 5

- D22 (Q21): Prototype input is synthetic demonstration data only; show the exact pre-capture warning recorded in the official plan and make no automatic sensitive-data detection claim. Explicit observation deletion removes all application-managed content depending exclusively on it and recalculates affected views. An optional content-free marker retains only random observation ID, deletion timestamp, and action—never text, excerpts, values, or a content hash. Deletion overrides provenance for that observation and does not claim erasure from previous exports, external copies, OS backups, or forensic recovery. See ADR 0004.
- D23 (Q22): Use this bounded privacy claim: "All application AI inference and workspace storage remain on the same computer. The application sends no observations to cloud inference services and includes no application telemetry." Rely on the OS user account; do not claim enterprise/multi-user authorization, database encryption, unlocked-computer protection, or production readiness. Ordinary logs exclude note/claim content; only synthetic evaluation prompts/outputs may be published. No automatic uploads. If using HTTP, bind to loopback, restrict intended origins, never bind to `0.0.0.0`/LAN, and stop the service with the application where possible. Validate offline operation and inspect observed network activity without claiming complete OS security.
- D24 (Q23; resolves D6 export timing): An explicit action generates one versioned JSON export locally while offline. It includes schema/export metadata and the complete synthetic workspace evidence/reconciliation relationships with stable references. No upload/transmission, import, CRM compatibility, or collaboration is implemented or claimed. Warn before export that later local deletion cannot remove prior export content. JSON is authoritative for MVP; CSV is later-only.
- D25 (Q24): QVAC claims remain drafts until explicit review and cannot affect views, records, discrepancies, or aggregates. Deterministically validate schema/enums, types/ranges, cited evidence/reference existence, subject existence, required quantity/location rules, and relationships, while stating these checks do not prove semantic support. Review shows claim, excerpt, subject/group, quantity scope, source, certainty, and proposed asset relationship together. The user may accept, correct, reject, or leave unresolved. Only accepted or human-authored claims enter the working view. Acceptance confirms interpretation of the available statement, not factual truth. Evaluate and report semantic correctness, unsupported interpretations, corrections, and residual reviewed errors separately; never equate excerpt presence with grounding or claim zero residual error.
- D26 (Q25): Persist one observation ID before inference. Each attempt records ID, observation ID/revision, start time, and Processing/Succeeded/Failed/Interrupted/Stale status. Startup changes lingering Processing attempts to Interrupted. Retry reuses the observation, creates a new attempt, and replaces/invalidates earlier drafts. Apply results only for the active attempt/current revision with no later superseding manual or accepted work; manual correction increments revision and invalidates older in-flight results. Activate accepted claims and update the view transactionally. E5 tests interruption, manual work before late completion, and two retries. Use revision and attempt records, not event sourcing.

## Explicit assumptions and evidence limits

- A1: No Philips employee or healthcare sales representative has been interviewed. Capture timing, existing CRM processes, reporting obligations, and willingness to adopt are unvalidated.
- A2: Avoiding personal notes followed by restructuring or re-entry is an intended benefit, not a measured result. Mandatory rewriting elsewhere could undermine adoption.
- A3: Philips may have installed-base governance, but its ownership and rules are unknown. The proposed verification responsibility is a prototype choice.
- A4 (source resolved after round 2): The supplied original challenge brief has been inspected and copied unchanged to [references/PHILIPS_CHALLENGE_BRIEF.docx](references/PHILIPS_CHALLENGE_BRIEF.docx), with a matching SHA-256 recorded in [reference notes](references/README.md). It supports the broad problem framing about scattered knowledge and capture burden. It does not empirically establish the chosen workflow, persona, adoption incentive, or verification ownership.
- A5: The below time and quality goals are targets, not achieved measurements.
- A6: A production deployment would require an approved synchronization or CRM integration mechanism; its ownership, security model, and business process are unknown and outside MVP scope.
- A7: An equipment-only note may provide sufficient personal value through history and verification reminders despite remaining additional work. This is not established by narrowing the persona or by the challenge brief.
- A8: User-provided Track 01 publication text states on-device/permitted-peer QVAC, disqualifying cloud inference, and flexible interfaces. It is preserved in `docs/references/TRACK_01_USER_PROVIDED_TEXT.md` but remains unverified until an exact organizer source is stored.
- A9: The five-minute video, public repository, Apache-2.0/permissive-license expectation, structured results, component disclosure, exact SDK-interface requirement, deadline/portal, and judging rubric are self-imposed or unknown—not verified Philips-track rules—until E8 resolves them.
