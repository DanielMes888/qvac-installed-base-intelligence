# Preserve evidence scope separately from asset identity

Accepted during grill-with-docs, round 1 (Q4-Q5). Keep observed groups, claims about unidentified members, identified equipment records, and reported totals distinct: neither a group observation nor a total establishes individual asset identities, and a claim about one unidentified member must not spread to all members. This rejects the simpler approach of forcing every claim into an identified equipment record and presenting one inventory count; it preserves useful partial evidence at the cost of more complex representation, reconciliation, and display.

Round 2 refinement (Q7-Q9): replace the ambiguous identified-asset category with verified individual assets, provisional individual assets, and unlinked equipment claims. Display verified assets, provisional assets, and reported totals separately; never combine them into one authoritative count. Calculate discrepancies only for comparable scopes and time bases, retain competing totals visibly, and keep information source separate from claim certainty. Detailed identity validation, repeated provisional records, treatment of seeded groups, and time comparability rules remain open in the grilling record.

Round 3 refinement (Q11-Q15): verification in this MVP is a match to a trusted fictional seed reference, not syntax/uniqueness validation or proof of current installation. Unresolved observations do not create provisional records; record creation and provisional links require explicit decisions with reasons. Preserve original notes and separately attributed revisions, retain scope/time distinctions and history, and allow explicitly supported supersession without recency-based truth selection. A small evidence-entry model and explicit decisions suffice; do not introduce a general-purpose knowledge graph or full event-sourcing framework.
