# Attempt five remaining official Stretch Goals

Status: accepted by project owner on 2026-09-11

The stored Philips challenge brief lists eight optional Stretch Goals, separately from its Minimum Viable Prototype: Voice capture, Photo-assisted capture, Duplicate detection, Confidence scoring, Data freshness, AI follow-up questions, Natural-language analytics, and Opportunity identification. The bounded emergency prototype already demonstrates conservative duplicate detection, data freshness, and one limited AI follow-up question; Confidence scoring is only partial because certainty states, verification priority, and candidate-match scores do not provide the brief's explainable assessment based on completeness, recency, and independent corroboration.

The project owner authorizes a voluntary attempt to complete the five unfinished goals: explainable Confidence scoring, bounded Opportunity identification, evidence-backed Natural-language analytics, Photo-assisted capture with fully local OCR, and Voice capture with fully local transcription. This extension strengthens the demonstration but is not a minimum challenge requirement, does not complete or unblock the twelve formal tickets, and will receive separate tickets only after the owner approves the implementation plan. Cross-device synchronization is excluded because it is not an official Stretch Goal and remains deferred under [ADR 0002](0002-limit-mvp-to-one-local-workspace.md).

The extension preserves the emergency core authorized by [ADR 0006](0006-use-time-constrained-browser-prototype.md), prompt v9, the selected model and adapter, conservative reconciliation, synthetic-only data, same-computer processing, no cloud or delegated inference, no telemetry or automatic upload, and the non-production privacy boundary. E4 and E4-v2 remain failed and unchanged. Voice and photo/OCR must begin with bounded feasibility checks covering license, size, memory, runtime compatibility, and offline execution; if no viable local path exists, work stops and the owner decides whether to defer or approve a separately bounded alternative.

Full manual rehearsal is deferred until the five-feature extension and the planned UI/UX integration are complete. Each feature still requires automated tests and its own smoke before integration. Release, reproducibility, competition-compliance, and production-readiness claims remain gated separately.

Owner closure note, 2026-09-11: the initial Confidence scoring rule is configurable and explainable, with 40 points for completeness, 30 for freshness, and 30 for independent corroboration. One Observation and human review alone cannot earn independent-corroboration points, and the score remains separate from certainty, verification priority, acceptance, and verified identity. Opportunity identification is restricted to explainable evidence-backed possibility signals and cannot assert purchase intent, clinical need, obsolescence, or a commercial recommendation. Natural-language analytics uses a separate read-only contract and prompt without changing `prototype-equipment-extraction-v9`; QVAC may interpret intent locally, while deterministic allowlisted code produces results and rejects or requests reformulation outside the contract.

Photo/OCR and voice retain only reviewed and submitted text as Observation content, respectively marked with `photo-assisted` and `voice` provenance. Original images and audio are temporary and are discarded after review, submission, cancellation, or failure. Neither media path may claim label authenticity or unambiguous identity. Exact scoring and opportunity thresholds, analytics contract details, and local OCR/transcription technology choices remain implementation-ticket decisions subject to their tests and feasibility stop conditions.

Amendment note, 2026-09-11: the owner separately authorized a **Geographic Installed-Base Map** because the stored brief lists it under Expected Output. This does not change the decision to attempt five unfinished Stretch Goals and does not create a ninth Stretch Goal. The map is a local/offline, synthetic-only, read-only demonstration experience with a schematic or owned visualization, an accessible hierarchy, allowlisted geographic/modality aggregates, and Customer 360 navigation. It excludes remote tiles, geocoding, external services, real Philips locations, invented geographic precision, QVAC inference, and Equipment Record mutation. E4/E4-v2 and the original ticket states remain unchanged.
