## What the brief establishes

- **Challenge Statement / The Problem:** customer-facing employees collect equipment knowledge that can remain scattered in notes, conversations, and memory. Capture burden, uncertainty, and repeated observations motivate the challenge. These are attributable problem statements, not validation of our selected persona or workflow.
- **Your Mission / Minimum Viable Prototype:** conversational observation capture, AI extraction, structured storage, a customer-level installed-base view, and basic aggregation or visualization across customers.
- **Conversational Data Capture:** multiple interface forms are allowed, including a Teams agent, mobile/web application, voice assistant, or chatbot. A physical phone is not mandated here.
- **Minimum Viable Prototype:** use a small synthetic dataset; do not use confidential customer information or real competitive information. The brief calls for fictional customers, manufacturers, equipment models, and locations where appropriate. The project's exact treatment of brand/model names remains to be settled.
- **Design Considerations:** capture simplicity, employee adoption, security/privacy, access controls, and responsible data governance matter. No specific access-control mechanism or numerical performance threshold is supplied.
- **The Hackathon Prototype:** a production-ready enterprise system is not required; the objective is an end-to-end concept.
- **Intelligent Validation / Stretch Goals:** follow-up questions and duplicate/conflict handling are suggested capabilities. The project's official plan intentionally makes some of these stronger commitments.
